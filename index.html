Smart City Resource, Transport and Emergency Optimization

Three algorithms from the DAA syllabus working on city problems. Change the inputs and run them.

Emergency routing (Dijkstra)

Finds the shortest travel time between two places. Roads work in both directions.

Press the button to see the route.

Budget allocation (0/1 Knapsack DP)

Chooses the projects that give the most impact without going over the budget.

Press the button to see the best selection.

Vehicle dispatch (Activity Selection, greedy)

Schedules the largest number of emergency tasks that do not overlap.

Press the button to see the schedule.
Algorithms: Dijkstra (Unit IV), 0/1 Knapsack dynamic programming (Unit III), Activity Selection (Unit II).
